Pitbox Helper - pit, tyre and fuel overlay (CSP Lua app)

Install: drag PitboxHelper.zip onto Content Manager and click Install
(or copy apps/lua/PitboxHelper into <Assetto Corsa>/apps/lua/). Needs Custom Shaders Patch.

Top: pit limiter state (flashes red if you're in the pit lane with it off),
status (ON TRACK / PIT LANE / IN BOX / BOX THIS LAP), speed (in the pit lane: speed over the limit).

Tyres: wear remaining % above, inner/middle/outer temps below, core temp, pressure and laps on the set.
Stripes: blue = cold, white = in the window, amber = warm, red = overheating.
The window is the car's optimum tyre temp (or your own in settings) +/- 12 C by default.

Fuel: burn is averaged over your last 5 laps (laps where you refuelled are skipped).
"Add at stop" = fuel to finish the race plus the margin, minus what's in the tank.
Race length comes from the session (laps, or time left / your lap time), or set it in settings.

Warnings: overheating / hot / cold tyres, big inner-outer spread, worn tyres, hot brakes, low fuel.
"BOX THIS LAP" flashes when fuel won't last 1.3 more laps or a tyre is below half the wear warning.
Settings (cog on the app bar): units (C/F, km/h / mph, psi/bar), temp window, warning levels, race laps, margin.
